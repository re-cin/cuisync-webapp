Privacy Policy

How CuiSync collects, uses and protects your personal information, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Last updated: November 2026

SECTIONS

Section 1

WHO WE ARE

CuiSync is a research web app that finds similar dishes across Asian cuisines based on their ingredients and cooking steps. It is run by the CuiSync research team. In this policy, “we” means the CuiSync team and “you” means anyone who uses the app.

Section 2

YOU CAN SEARCH WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

Searching by text, tags or photo works without an account. If you search without logging in, we don’t link your searches to you.

Section 3

WHAT WE COLLECT WHEN YOU CREATE AN ACCOUNT

  • Account details: your email address, username and (optional) display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Password: handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase). It is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form; we can never see it.
  • Search history: the dishes you searched for, the country you chose and the result, so you can open them again from your profile.
  • Food preferences you choose to add: what kind of cook you are, your home country, favourite cuisines, allergens to watch for and a short bio.
  • Consent record: the date and time you agreed to this policy.
Section 4

PHOTOS YOU UPLOAD

When you use Search by Photo Text, the photo is sent to our text-reading (OCR) service to read the dish names. The photo is used only for that search and is not saved.

Section 5

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To create your account, let you log in and keep it secure (for example, confirmation and password emails).
  • To show your search history and use your food preferences, such as pointing out the allergens you watch for.
  • To see, in totals only, how the app is used (for example, number of searches per day) for our research.

We don’t sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Section 6

SERVICES THAT HELP RUN CUISYNC

  • Supabase: stores accounts, profiles and search history, and sends account emails.
  • Render: hosts the website.
  • Hugging Face: runs the text-reading service for photo searches.
  • OpenAI: writes the explanations and dish background shown with results. Only dish information is sent, never your account details.
  • USDA FoodData Central: provides nutrient information for ingredients. Only ingredient names are sent.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”.
Section 7

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep your account and search history until you delete them. You can clear your search history or delete your whole account at any time. When you delete your account, your profile and search history are deleted permanently.

Section 8

HOW WE PROTECT IT

Connections to CuiSync are encrypted (https). Passwords are hashed by Supabase, new accounts are confirmed by email, account changes need your password, and repeated wrong passwords are slowed down. Only the CuiSync team’s admin accounts can see the user list.

Section 9

YOUR RIGHTS

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erase or block, and to get a copy of your personal information, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. In CuiSync you can:

  • See and correct your name, username and food preferences on your profile page.
  • Change your email or password in Profile › Account and password.
  • Delete your account and all its data in Profile › Account and password › Delete account.
